## The plan's changelog entry, checked against the tree

The plan's Task 12 draft was written before any of Tasks 1–13 ran. Every claim in it was checked
against `src/` and `test/` on disk. Six corrections:

1. **The wind plaque's third state was missing.** The draft named calm and "no reported bearing".
   `renderWindPlot` has a third branch: an unreported wind clears the `<g>` entirely and leaves
   the bare compass ring, because a station circle would assert a reading nobody took. Added.
2. **Task 11's spec behaviour was absent, because the plan omitted it everywhere.** The direction
   line reads `variable`, `no direction`, or `unreported` in `--muted` (`.note.no-heading`,
   toggled by `renderWindDirection`). Task 11 found the plan never mentioned it and implemented
   it. The changelog now names the line and its quieter colour.
3. **The directionless ring was in the plan's README step but not its changelog step.** The button
   draws `DIRECTIONLESS_RING` in the force colour for a wind with a speed and no bearing
   (`drawWindRing` in `src/button-icon.js`). Added to the button entry.
4. **"Coverage as width" was true but incomplete.** `COVERS` in `src/cloud-sky.js` sets both a
   puff count and a width fraction, and OVC is not a puff row at all — `overcastLid` draws a solid
   lid. `renderSky` also draws the dashed base on a clear sky, which is the case where it is the
   only cloud figure on the plaque. Both stated.
5. **The button's chip colour was never mentioned.** The draft said only that the comfort colour
   moved to a band. The bigger visible change is that the face itself became Kit's toolbar-field
   indigo (`CHIP_INK`), with the reading in chrome text. Stated, and "on every path" added to the
   trend notch, because the band and the notch no longer move for a notable wind — only the
   figures give way.
6. **The trend glyph became a shape on both surfaces.** `TREND_GLYPHS` (canvas polygons) and
   `TREND_POINTS` (SVG polygons) replaced the arrow characters the popup used to write into its
   pressure line, and `describeTrend` no longer carries one. Not in the draft at all; added as its
   own Changed entry.

Two smaller additions of the same kind: the barometer's needle is clamped to the 980–1050 hPa
scale (`BAROMETER` + `clamp` in `src/popup.js`), and `toCloudLayers` drops a layer the station
reports with no `base`.

Nothing in the draft was found to be outright wrong. Everything above is a claim that was thinner
than the code.

## README

The button section was rewritten around three facts that the old text got backwards:

- The old text said "The button shows no wind direction: sixteen bearings do not survive three
  device pixels". The dart is a bearing, so that whole paragraph went.
- The old text said a notable wind takes the bottom band and the trend moves to a top-right
  corner mark. `drawButtonIcon` draws the band and the trend unconditionally now; the reading is
  the only thing that gives way.
- The windsock is gone from the tree, so it is gone from the prose.
  `grep -rni 'windsock\|sock' README.md docs/*.md docs/*.html docs/*.js` returns nothing.

The popup section was rewritten from a six-item list of fields into the panel's actual structure:
header, four plaques, thunder strip, footer. Each plaque names its instrument.

Written under the `simple-english` skill (ASD-STE100, pragmatic mode). British `colour` was kept
against the standard's Rule 1.14, because the whole repo and every identifier in `src/` spell it
that way and Rule 9.4 (one consistent terminology) is the stronger constraint here.
