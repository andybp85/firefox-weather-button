## Deviation from the plan

The plan's Step 1 test asserted the directionless ring's colour is `#6cc8f7` for the fixture
`{ gustKnots: 21, knots: 6, state: 'measured' }`. That hex is Beaufort force 1 (light air, 1–3 kt), which matches
neither the sustained 6 kt nor the announced 21 kt. The spec (`Resolved decisions` §2 and the `Toolbar button`
section) says the ring is drawn "in the force colour", and the force colour is
`beaufortForce(announcedKnots(wind))`. The gust is 15 kt over the sustained, so the announced speed is 21 kt →
force 5 → `#c8f640`. The spec wins over the plan on geometry and colour, so the shipped test asserts `#c8f640`,
with a comment naming the arithmetic. The same correction was written back into the plan's Step 1 snippet so the
plan does not carry a known-wrong number into a later review.

No other departure from the plan's or the spec's numbers.

## Step 6 — the preview

The environment has no browser, so the visual check was replaced by a numeric one of the property the step exists to
catch (the dart's sense). `dartPoints` about `(32, 25)` at scale 1:

| Bearing from | Tip     | Reads as    |
| ------------ | ------- | ----------- |
| 0 (N)        | 32, 41  | points down |
| 90 (E)       | 16, 25  | points left |
| 180 (S)      | 32, 9   | points up   |
| 270 (W)      | 48, 25  | points right |

That is the downwind convention the spec asks for: a wind from the north flies down the face, and the sweep will
read as a clock face. The 16 px legibility question the step also raises is already deferred to the verification
bean by the spec's `Resolved decisions` §4.

The preview's twelve cases were also run through `toWind`/`describeWind` to confirm each row's label is the wording
intended: `unreported`, `calm`, `NE 14 kt`, `NE 15 kt`, `SSW 15 kt`, `WNW 22 kt G 31`, `W 18 kt G 32`,
`S 55 kt G 65`, `variable 6 kt G 21`, `NNE 8 kt`, `unreported`, `SSE 4 kt`.
